How to Interpret the Score

The indicator equals 100 × passed checks ÷ applicable checks, rounded. A pass means the static HTML satisfied one specific rule, not that the entire experience is good. Some checks may be excluded when a category is switched off.

Review each finding in context. Automated static checks cannot confirm user satisfaction, SEO rankings, screen-reader usability, contrast across all states, responsive rendering or page speed.

Uploaded and pasted HTML is processed locally. If you choose to fetch a URL, your browser connects directly to that website; MiniToolsDeck does not proxy or store its contents.
