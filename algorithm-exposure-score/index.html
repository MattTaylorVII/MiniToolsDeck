What This Tool Measures

Concentration of income across platform-dependent streams, with scenario-based loss estimates. The score is a transparent index (0–100), not the probability of an algorithm change, bankruptcy or income loss.

We cannot predict algorithm changes, measure audience overlap, verify revenue inputs, or know whether platform reach and income move proportionally. Model your own assumptions using the sliders.

Revenue assumptions and imported CSV files are analysed in your browser. Your business data is not uploaded to MiniToolsDeck.
