Interpretation

Statistical Methodology

Two-sided pooled two-proportion z-test for the null hypothesis of equal conversion rates. The difference interval uses an unpooled normal approximation; individual rates use Wilson intervals. The allocation diagnostic is a two-sided normal approximation to the binomial split.

A p-value is not the probability that B is better. Statistical significance is not practical importance. Results may be unreliable with small counts, selection bias, tracking errors, sequential testing or overlapping visitors. Use one primary metric and a predeclared stopping rule.

Calculations and CSV processing run locally in your browser. No campaign data is uploaded to a server.
