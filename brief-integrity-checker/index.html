How the Assessment Works

A structured comparison, not AI judgement. Each of the eight fields contributes equally: identical normalized entries count as aligned; different entries require review; missing entries are incomplete. Score = 100 × (aligned + 0.5 × review needed) ÷ 8. A high score is not legal approval or proof of mutual understanding.

Differences in wording may represent the same intent. Review every flagged item with both parties before treating the brief as agreed. Marking the review checkbox records a declaration, not an electronic signature.
