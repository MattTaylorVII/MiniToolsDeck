Methodology & Limitations

The index is a transparent heuristic, not a prediction or independently validated reliability rating. It begins at 100 and deducts up to 25 points per dimension based on affected-record rates. Unmapped fields are not tested and are shown as unassessed; the score is based only on available checks.

No identity resolution, bot detection, causal attribution, conversion lift or revenue impact is inferred from these records. Investigate findings against source systems before changing budgets.
