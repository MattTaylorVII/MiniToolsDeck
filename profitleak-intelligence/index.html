02 · Accounting Context

Currency changes the display symbol only (no exchange-rate conversion). Optional operating expenses for the same period as the imported data (e.g., rent and software). Not allocated to channels.

How We Calculate Profit

Net sales = gross sales − discounts − refunds. Pre-marketing contribution = net sales − cost of goods − shipping/fulfillment − payment/transaction fees. Modeled profit = pre-marketing contribution − imported ad spend − additional period costs. Returns do not automatically restock inventory, and attribution is not established from a channel label. Missing costs make this a partial, not an audited profit statement.
