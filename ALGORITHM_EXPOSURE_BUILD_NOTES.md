Algorithm Exposure Score — first flagship build

- Integrated homepage link, sitemap and redirect from retired Engagement Calculator.
- No external AI or backend. CSV parsing, calculation, exports operate locally.
- Score: 100 × sqrt(sum of squared shares for platform-dependent revenue streams).
- Shock loss = selected platform revenue × min(1, reach decline × revenue sensitivity).
- Diversification shifts a selected percentage of selected platform revenue to another source, assumes immediate equivalent revenue replacement without cost.
- All estimates are scenarios, not causal claims or predictions.
- Browser QA and accessibility review recommended before production deployment.
